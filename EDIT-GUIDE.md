# How to change your website (for Mr Night)

You change your website on your phone. You do not need a computer.

**Open the editor:** open **app.pagescms.org** in Chrome → sign in with **GitHub** → tap your site → tap **My website**.
After every change, tap **Save**. Your website changes in about **1 minute**.

---

## 1. Change a price

1. Tap **Trips and prices**.
2. Tap **Featured trip cards** (the big cards) or **Price table rows** (the list).
3. Tap the trip, for example **Temple day**.
4. In **Price per van**, type the number only. Example: `45` (not "$45").
5. Tap **Save**.

Leave the price **empty** if you want people to message you. The website then shows **"Message me for price"**.

---

## 2. Add a photo

1. Tap **My photos**.
2. Tap the photo you want to add, for example **My van – inside**.
3. Tap **Upload** and choose a photo from your phone.
4. Tap **Save**.

Good photos: you, your van outside, inside, the massage seats, you with guests.
Only use photos of guests if they say yes.

---

## 3. When the Thai border opens again

Right now the land border is closed, so the **Coming from Bangkok** part is switched **off**. Nothing about Bangkok buses, Poipet or the border shows on your website. Your text is kept safe.

To show it again:

1. Tap **Coming from Bangkok**.
2. Switch on **Show this section**. (This also shows the border message and the Poipet steps.)
3. Tap **Thai border (Poipet)** and change **Border message**. Example: "The border at Poipet is open again. Message me to plan your trip."
4. In **Date of this update**, type today's date. Example: `20 Dec 2026`.
5. Tap **Save**.

Please only write what is true today. If you are not sure, use:
"Please message me to check the current border status before you travel."

The **Flying in** part (airport pick-up) stays on. You can change its text, the list of direct flights and the button there.

---

## 4. Add a link

In any text box (for example an answer in **Questions and answers**), write:

`[words to click](https://www.example.com/)`

Example: `[angkorenterprise.gov.kh](https://www.angkorenterprise.gov.kh/)`

Website addresses that start with `https://`, names like `example.com` and email addresses also become links by themselves. Website links open in a new tab.

---

## 5. Change the website name

1. Tap **My website** (the top of the form).
2. **Website / brand name** is the name in the logo, the page title and the WhatsApp chat box. Now it is `Mr Night · Siem Reap Driver & Guide`. On Google Maps, use only **Mr Night** as the business name (Google does not allow extra words in the name).
3. **My name in the text and messages** is how you are called in the text, for example `Mr Night`. Keep it short.
4. Tap **Save**.

---

## 6. The translate button

The round globe button at the top lets visitors read your site in Chinese, Korean, French or Japanese (Google Translate does this automatically).

- To hide it: tap **Translate menu** and switch off **Show the translate menu**.
- To change the languages: change **Languages**. Use codes with commas, for example `zh-CN,ko,fr,ja`.

Your name, phone number and WhatsApp messages stay the same in every language.

---

## Good to know

- **Reviews:** switch them on only when you have **real** reviews. Never write a review yourself. Paste the link to your real page (it starts with `https://`).
- **Google rating:** type your real rating (for example `4.9`) and your real number of reviews (for example `12`).
- **Your WhatsApp number** is in **WhatsApp number**. Numbers only: `85517843878`.
- If something looks wrong, ask your helper (the person who set up your website).

## Places and photos
- **Places I can take you**: in Pages CMS open *Places I can take you (destination cards)*. Each place has a name, one line of text and a photo. The green "Ask" button sends a WhatsApp message with the place name in it. If you use a photo that is not yours, ask your helper to add the credit to the photo credits page.
- **Photos from my trips**: open *Photo gallery (your own photos)*. Add your own photo and write a short caption, for example "Bayon temple with my guests".
