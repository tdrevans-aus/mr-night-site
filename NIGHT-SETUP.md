# Night's setup: scripts and ready-made texts

For you (the helper) to use with Night, one step at a time. Night works only from his phone, so everything here is something he can tap through or copy and paste. Do the steps in order and don't send the next one until the last one is done.

**Order:**
1. Licence photo (sent already)
2. Google Business Profile, on a video call together (section A)
3. WhatsApp Business (section B)
4. After every trip: the review request (section C)

---

## A. Google Business Profile: call script (about 30–45 minutes)

**Before the call, ask Night to have ready:**
- His phone, charged, with Wi-Fi
- The Gmail password for **mrnighttourguide@gmail.com**
- His van nearby, plus his guide licence card (Google may ask for a short video as proof)

**On the call, walk him through these steps. Ask him to read out each screen as he goes.**

1. **Open Chrome and go to** `business.google.com`. Tap **Get started** or **Manage now**.
   - If it asks which account: choose **mrnighttourguide@gmail.com**.
2. **Business name:** type exactly **Mr Night**. Nothing else.
   - Don't add "Siem Reap", "Driver" or "Tour Guide" to the name. Google doesn't allow extra words in business names and can suspend the profile for it. The extra words go in the category and description below.
3. **Business category:** type **Tour** and pick **Tour operator**.
   - If Google offers **Tour agency** or **Tourist information center** instead, choose the closest match to "tours". You can add more categories later (see step 10).
4. **"Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"** → **No**.
   - He drives to his customers, so he's a "service-area business". This keeps his home address private.
5. **Service areas:** type **Siem Reap** and choose it. Add **Siem Reap Province** too if Google offers it.
6. **Contact details:**
   - Phone: **+855 17 843 878**
   - Website: the site address once it's final. For now, the preview address or **skip**. Add it later.
7. **Verification:** Google decides how. Usually it's a **short video**: one continuous recording that shows:
   - the street sign or local area, with Night in Siem Reap
   - the van, inside and out
   - his guide licence card
   - him using the business (for example, opening the van door for a guest)

   If Google offers a phone call or text instead, take it. Verification can take a few days. Tell him that's normal.
8. **Business description:** paste this. It's 638 characters, under Google's 750 limit, and has no web links, which Google doesn't allow here.

   > Private driver and licensed Siem Reap tour guide with a Toyota Alphard luxury van (up to 6 guests, reclining seats, cold water and air-con). Airport pick-ups from the new Siem Reap Angkor International Airport (SAI), sunrise and full temple days at Angkor Wat, Bayon, Ta Prohm and Banteay Srei, Phnom Kulen waterfall, Beng Mealea, Tonle Sap floating village, golf transfers and hotel transfers. 15+ years as a guide in Siem Reap. I speak English, Thai and Khmer. I am your driver and your guide in one: I drive you, and I guide you inside the temples. One price for the whole van, not per person. Message me on WhatsApp to plan your trip.

9. **Photos:** add 10 of his own photos from his phone's gallery:
   - the van outside, the seats inside
   - him at Angkor Wat
   - guests at temples
   - the airport pick-up
   - golf

   No stock photos here.
10. **Extra categories** (Edit profile → Business category → Add another): **Tour agency** and **Tourist information center**, if they exist.
11. **Opening hours:** leave empty for now. The site deliberately shows no times.
12. **Get the review link:** on the profile, tap **Ask for reviews** (or **Share review form**) and **copy the link**. Ask him to send it to you on WhatsApp, and save it here:

    Review link: ______________________

**After the call:**
- When the profile has 3–5 real reviews, switch on the Google badge in Pages CMS: `reviews.google`, turn **show** on, paste the profile link, and type his rating and review count.
- Add the profile link to `google_maps_url` in Pages CMS too. The "Open in Google Maps" button then appears on the site.

## B. WhatsApp Business: setup and ready-made texts

**Before he switches:** WhatsApp Business is a separate free app that uses the **same number**. His chats move across if he backs up first (WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat backup → Back up). Do the backup with him.

1. **Install WhatsApp Business** from the Play Store or App Store. Open it, use the same number and restore the backup.
2. **Business name:** **Mr Night · Siem Reap Driver & Guide**
3. **Category:** Travel & Transportation (or "Tour agency" if offered)
4. **Description:**
   > Licensed Siem Reap guide · Toyota Alphard luxury van · SAI airport pick-ups · Temple days · Kulen waterfall · Golf. English, Thai, Khmer.
5. **Email:** mrnighttourguide@gmail.com
6. **Website:** the site address, once final
7. **Profile photo:** his headshot. The van is in the photos; his face is the trust signal.

### Greeting message
*(Business tools → Greeting message. Sent automatically to new customers.)*

> Hello, thank you for messaging Mr Night! 🙏
> I am a private driver and licensed Siem Reap guide with a Toyota Alphard van.
> To plan your trip, please tell me:
> 1. Your dates
> 2. How many people (and how many big suitcases)
> 3. Your hotel
> 4. Your flight number, if you need an airport pick-up
> I will reply as soon as I can with a clear price for the whole van.

### Away message
*(Business tools → Away message. Use "Outside of business hours" or "Always send", as he prefers.)*

> Thank you for your message! I am driving or guiding at the moment, so I can't reply right now. I will reply as soon as I can. 🙏
> Mr Night

### Quick replies
*(Business tools → Quick replies. He types "/" and the shortcut to insert the text.)*

- **/price**
  > Thank you! For [dates] and [number] people, the price for the whole van is US$___. This includes fuel, cold water and me as your driver and licensed guide. Temple tickets (Angkor Pass) are not included. Shall I book it for you?
- **/airport**
  > I will meet you at arrivals at SAI airport with a sign with your name. Please send me your flight number. I watch your flight, so if it is late I will still be there. The drive to Siem Reap town is about 1 hour.
- **/pass**
  > The Angkor Pass is bought at the official ticket office (Angkor Enterprise) or online at angkorenterprise.gov.kh. I can take you to the ticket office on the way. You need it for the main temples.
- **/thanks**: the review request in section C.

### Labels
*(Chats → long-press a chat → Label.)* Create four labels and move each chat along:

**New enquiry** → **Price sent** → **Booked** → **Review asked**

Once a month, count the chats in each label. That shows how many enquiries turn into bookings.

## C. After every trip: the review request

Send this the **same evening**, while the trip is fresh. It's also the **/thanks** quick reply.

> Thank you for travelling with me today! 🙏 I hope you enjoyed Siem Reap.
> If you have one minute, a short Google review helps my family's small business a lot:
> [paste review link]
> Thank you! Mr Night

**Rules, so Google doesn't remove reviews:**
- Never offer anything in return for a review: no discount, gift or free water.
- Ask every guest, not just the happy ones.
- Never write reviews for himself, or ask family or friends to.
