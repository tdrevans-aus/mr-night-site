      Border update

          How the Poipet border crossing works
          1. Bus from Bangkok to Aranyaprathet (about 4–5 hours).
          2. Thai exit stamp, then walk across into Cambodia.
          3. Cambodia visa: get your e-visa only at evisa.gov.kh.
          4. About 150 km to Siem Reap (2.5–3 hours).
          Please check the border status with me before you book. Ask Mr Night
